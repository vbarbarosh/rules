# Priority quadrant: what to do first

Many pieces of work weighed on two things only: how much each is worth
and how much it costs. Value goes up, effort goes right, and the square
splits in four quarters: do first, plan, later, skip. The reader sees
what to start with and what to drop.

![Eight checkout ideas on value and effort: the green Do first quarter, high value and low effort, holds Apple Pay and saved cards; the blue Plan quarter, high value and high effort, holds the one-page checkout and tax by country; the grey Later quarter, low value and low effort, holds the coupon field and the receipt e-mail; the dashed grey Skip quarter, low value and high effort, holds dark mode and gift wrap as hollow dots; a Now box says Apple Pay and saved cards come first and gift wrap and dark mode are dropped](../img/diagrams/quadrant_checkout.svg)

## Fits when

- **Many ideas and room for a few,** and the question is which ones to
  start with.
- **Two things decide it:** what a piece of work brings and what it
  costs, each a rough guess, not a measure.
- **The reader must say no** to some of them, and wants to see why.

## Not when

- **Work by stage today,** in flight or stuck: a
  [kanban board](kanban.md).
- **The months ahead,** what is planned when: a [roadmap](roadmap.md).
- **Options compared on many criteria,** not two: a table, a row per
  option and a column per criterion.

## How it is drawn

1. **The axes:** value up the left side, effort along the bottom, each
   an arrow with its name; no numbers, only more and less.
2. **The quarters:** four framed boxes with a gap between them, each
   named in its outer corner in bold, what it means under the name.
   Do first is green, top left; plan is blue, top right; later is grey,
   bottom left; skip is grey with a dashed border, bottom right.
3. **The dots:** one per piece of work, where its value and effort put
   it, in its quarter's colour; a skipped one is hollow. Four to twelve
   of them.
4. **The labels:** the name in bold on its own light pill beside its
   dot, clear of the quarter's border and of other pills; right of the
   dot, or left when the right has no room.
5. **Now:** a blue box under the panel says in one sentence what comes
   first and what is dropped.
