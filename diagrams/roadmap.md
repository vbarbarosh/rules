# Roadmap: what is planned when

The months ahead on one line of time, a lane per area of work, each piece
of work a bar from its start to its end; a red line marks today. The
reader sees what is done, what is under way, what is at risk and what
comes next.

![The shop from July to December: Payments has the card form done and bank payouts under way; Checkout has Apple Pay under way and tax by country at risk in amber after it; Help centre has the gift card articles done and search planned in grey; a red dashed line marks today, 10-16; a Now box says tax by country is at risk because it needs Apple Pay](../img/diagrams/roadmap_shop.svg)

## Fits when

- **Several areas of work over months:** what each one does when, side
  by side.
- **The question is what comes next** and what is late, measured against
  today.
- **One piece of work waits on another,** and the reader sees the delay
  push the next one.

## Not when

- **One goal and its steps,** done or not, no dates ahead: a
  [milestone track](milestone.md).
- **Work by stage today,** not by month: a [kanban board](kanban.md).
- **What already happened,** from a log: a [timeline](timeline.md).

## How it is drawn

1. **Time:** the months left to right in one framed panel, their names
   on top; four to twelve of them.
2. **Lanes:** one row per area of work, its name in bold on the left,
   thin lines between the rows; two to five lanes.
3. **Bars:** one per piece of work, from its start to its end, its name
   inside in two or three words. Green is done, blue under way, amber at
   risk with its name in amber, grey with a dashed border planned.
4. **Today:** a red dashed line down the panel, behind the bars, its date
   on a red pill at the bottom.
5. **Now:** a blue box under the panel says in one sentence what is
   under way and what is at risk, and why.
