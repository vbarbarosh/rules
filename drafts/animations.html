Animations shown rather than described: each one has a name to use in a message, a mockup to try it on, and the rules it follows.

1 · Slide, like workspaces

slide-workspaces

Switching between sidebar items moves the view the way the selection moves. The highlight slides from the old item to the new one, and the content follows it: going down the list, the old content leaves upward and the new content rises from below. Going up the list, it is the reverse. This is how GNOME switches workspaces.

  • Direction follows the list. Down the list (2 to 10): the old content goes up, the new one comes from below. Up the list (8 to 7): the reverse.
  • A far jump is one slide. From 2 to 10 the content does not pass through 3 to 9: the two pages slide, and only the highlight crosses the items between them.
  • Together. The highlight and the content move at the same time, with the same duration and easing: cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1), fast at the start and settling at the end.
  • A click mid-way changes course. Everything continues from where it is: no jump back to the start, and no waiting for the slide to finish.

2 · Step into a row

slide-into-row

The content is a list of rows. Clicking a row steps into it: the list leaves to the left and the row's page comes in from the right, as if you moved right. Back steps out the other way. The sidebar still slides up and down, as in slide-workspaces, from the list or from a row's page.

  • In is right, out is left. A row's page comes from the right and the list leaves to the left. Back: the page goes out to the right and the list comes back from the left.
  • Sections are up and down, depth is left and right. The sidebar always slides vertically, wherever you are; only stepping into a row and back moves sideways.
  • The section's item steps out too. In a row's page, clicking its own section in the sidebar is Back.
  • Mid-way changes course here too. Back during the step in turns it around; a section picked during it carries both pages away together.

3 · A list that moves

list-changes

Rows come, go and change places, and the list shows each change as movement: a new row opens a gap and fades into it, a removed row fades out and the rows below close the gap, a moved row travels to its new place while the others make room. Nothing jumps.

    Duration Click a row to select it; × removes it.
    • Added: a gap opens. The rows below slide down to make room and the new row fades into the gap.
    • Removed: the gap closes. The row fades out where it stands and the rows below slide up into its place.
    • Moved: it travels. The row goes to its new place over the others while they shift to make room, all at once, with the same duration and easing: cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1).
    • Nothing jumps (UIM-01). The selected row is the one you work with: rows added or removed above it never move it on the screen, and the scroll position takes up the difference. The dashed line marks its place. Switch Nothing jumps off to see the jump it prevents. Only your own move of the selected row moves it, and the view follows it.
    • A change mid-way continues from where things stand. Every row starts its next move from where it is on the screen at that moment; five adds in a row all animate, and the rows never overlap at the end.

    4 · Open and fold

    expand-collapse

    Three messages, each either folded to one line or open in full: text only, with a picture, and a voice message whose three status lines, transcript and status pill all have a place in the line. Clicking a folded header opens its message, clicking the open one folds it back. Nothing pops, the parts morph: the avatar, the time, the lines and the pill each travel to their place in the other layout and take its size there; only what has no place there fades. The header clicked stays where it is.
