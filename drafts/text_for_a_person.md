# A text is written for a person

A text is written for the person who reads it. It is short, compact and
to the point, with no filler. This is the first rule of writing; the
rules after it say how particular documents are built.

```
✗  Hello! In this message I would like to let you know that, after
   looking into the matter carefully and trying quite a few things, I can
   now say that the build works again, as far as I can tell.

✓  The build works again.
```

- **Short:** as few words as the point needs.
- **Compact:** each thing said once, in its place.
- **To the point:** what the reader needs, and nothing else; the point
  comes first, not after an introduction.
- **No filler:** no greeting or preamble, no "I would like to let you
  know", no words that say nothing.

Every text a person reads is written so: a message, a reply, a README,
a page of the docs, a commit message.
